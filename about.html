About Me

There has never been just one thing that defines me.

I have been in the military, worked different jobs, become a barber, started my own business, and returned to school. I like that my background is varied because every experience has given me a different way of looking at work and what I want from my future.

A little about me

I learn best by doing.

I have never been afraid of taking a different route, trying something new, or figuring things out as I go. That mindset has followed me through the military, business ownership, and now SDSU.

My Background

I did not follow one straight line from school into a career. My path has included serving in the United States Air Force, working in different environments, becoming a barber, running my own business, and eventually returning to college. I think that variety is one of my strengths. It has put me around different people, different expectations, and different problems to solve, and it has helped me become comfortable adapting when things do not go exactly as planned.

Barbering & Owning a Business

Barbering started as a skill, but it also became my introduction to running a business for myself. I have learned how important relationships and reputation are when your clients can choose to go anywhere. I enjoy the conversations and the variety of people I meet, but I also like the business side of keeping things organized, managing my time, and figuring out how to keep clients coming back. It has made business feel real to me instead of something that only exists in a textbook.

Education & Professional Development

Photo by Alvin Vera, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. Source: Wikimedia Commons

After completing my associate degree in Business Administration, I transferred to San Diego State University to continue working toward my bachelor's degree. Going back to school has been important to me because I already had real-world experience with work and business, and I wanted to understand the bigger picture behind it. At SDSU, I am building on what I already know while preparing myself for opportunities that would have been harder to reach without finishing my degree.

Career Goals

Right now, I am interested in moving into management or operations because those areas fit the parts of work I enjoy most: solving problems, working with people, organizing what needs to get done, and taking responsibility for results. I do not expect my career to follow one perfectly planned route. I want to keep learning, take opportunities that make sense, and eventually use that experience to keep growing as both a professional and a business owner.

Outside of Work & School

I have always been someone who likes trying things and following whatever catches my interest. I am into cars, music, and random projects, and I have a habit of finding something interesting and wanting to figure it out for myself. I can be spontaneous, which has led to a few unexpected purchases and projects over the years, but I think that curiosity is also part of how I learn. I like understanding how things work, whether it is a business, a vehicle, or something I have never tried before.
