Selected Job Advertisement

Administrative & Operations Coordinator

Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial Association • San Diego, California

Full-time$22–$25/hourMonday–FridayIn person

Role Summary

The position supports the Director of Operations and combines office administration, customer and donor service, database and records management, correspondence, volunteer and event support, and general organizational coordination.

Selected Responsibilities

  • Respond to calls, emails, visitors, donors, members, and general inquiries.
  • Process veteran memorial plaque requests and maintain related records.
  • Update membership, donor, and organizational databases and maintain filing systems.
  • Prepare correspondence and materials for meetings.
  • Support volunteers, recognition activities, Memorial Day, Veterans Day, and other events.
  • Take ownership of special projects and changing day-to-day office needs.

Qualifications I Am Targeting

  • Strong organization and attention to detail.
  • Professional customer service and communication.
  • Microsoft Office skills and ability to learn database or CRM systems.
  • Ability to manage several responsibilities, use good judgment, and work independently.
  • Two or more years of administrative, business support, office, or customer service experience preferred; an associate degree in business or related field is a plus.

Tailored Cover Letter

Brice Camarena

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am interested in the Administrative & Operations Coordinator position with the Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial Association. As a U.S. Air Force veteran, small business owner, and Business Administration student at San Diego State University, I was immediately drawn to both the responsibilities of the position and the organization's mission of honoring veterans by preserving their legacy.

My background has given me experience that fits well with the day-to-day needs of this role. While serving in the Air Force with RED HORSE, I managed records and accountability for 293 government vehicles and pieces of equipment valued at more than $40 million. I prepared reports and correspondence, tracked inspections and deadlines, reviewed documentation for accuracy, and coordinated with several departments to keep operations moving. That experience taught me the importance of organization, attention to detail, and following through when other people are depending on the information I provide.

After leaving the military, I continued building those skills in civilian business operations. As an Operations Manager at Rogelio's Fine Foods, I handled purchasing, inventory, vendors, invoicing, scheduling, and daily operational issues. I later built my own mobile barber business, where I manage appointments, customer communication, purchasing, expenses, records, and long-term client relationships. Running a small business has made me comfortable wearing several hats, adjusting when priorities change, and taking responsibility for problems instead of waiting for someone else to solve them.

I would be proud to bring that combination of military service, customer service, and hands-on business experience to an organization whose mission has a personal connection for me. I am currently continuing my education at San Diego State University, and I see this type of position as a strong opportunity to contribute while continuing to grow in administrative operations and management.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience and background could support the Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial Association.

Sincerely,
Brice Camarena
