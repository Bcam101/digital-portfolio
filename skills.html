Job Skills

Skills developed through experience, not just coursework.

My strongest professional skills come from military service, managing operations, owning a small business, and continuing my education in Business Administration.

Operations & Organization

I learned to keep people, equipment, records, schedules, and priorities moving in environments where details matter.

Where I developed it

United States Air Force, Rogelio's Fine Foods, and my mobile barber business.

How I used it

In the Air Force I tracked vehicle readiness, inspections, work orders, records, and operational deadlines. Later I managed purchasing, inventory, scheduling, vendors, appointments, and day-to-day business needs.

Accountability & Attention to Detail

I am comfortable being responsible for information, property, and work that has to be accurate.

Where I developed it

820th RED HORSE Squadron, United States Air Force.

How I used it

I helped maintain accountability and documentation for 293 government vehicles and pieces of equipment valued at more than $40 million, while reviewing records and supporting readiness requirements.

Customer Service & Communication

Working directly with people has taught me how important reliability, communication, and problem solving are to a professional relationship.

Where I developed it

My mobile barber business and previous civilian operations work.

How I used it

I communicate with clients, coordinate appointments, resolve scheduling issues, build repeat business, and maintain long-term customer relationships. Running my own business means my reputation depends on how I handle every interaction.

Problem Solving & Adaptability

I have worked in very different environments, and each one has required me to adjust quickly and find practical solutions.

Where I developed it

Military service, operations management, business ownership, and college.

How I used it

I have coordinated across departments, resolved documentation and operational issues, handled changing schedules, managed unexpected business problems, and returned to school to continue developing professionally.

Business Operations

Owning and managing businesses has given me hands-on experience with the everyday decisions that keep an operation functioning.

Where I developed it

Rogelio's Fine Foods and my mobile barber business.

How I used it

I have managed purchasing, inventory, expenses, customer relationships, scheduling, financial records, equipment, and service delivery while making decisions based on efficiency and customer retention.

Administrative & Technical Skills

I have experience creating and maintaining the documentation that supports organized operations.

Where I developed it

Air Force service, civilian management, business ownership, and school.

How I used it

I have prepared reports and spreadsheets, maintained electronic and paper records, handled financial documentation, performed data entry, and used Microsoft Word, Excel, and Outlook.

What connects these skills: My career has not followed one straight path, but the same themes keep showing up: responsibility, organization, communication, adaptability, and taking ownership of the work in front of me.
