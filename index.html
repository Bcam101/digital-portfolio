The Experiences That Shaped Me

The things I have learned about work did not all come from a classroom. Military service, running my own barber business, and returning to school have each taught me something different.

Air Force

Military service taught me accountability, adaptability, and the importance of doing my part when other people are depending on me.

Barber & Business Owner

Barbering taught me that running a business is about much more than the service itself. It means managing clients, appointments, expenses, relationships, and my reputation every day.

SDSU

Returning to school and continuing my business education has given me a chance to connect what I have learned through experience with a stronger understanding of management and business.

Small Business Ownership

Being a Barber Taught Me Business by Doing It

Photo by MURSHID ELAMARAM, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Source: Wikimedia Commons

Becoming a barber gave me a side of business that I could never get from a textbook alone. The haircut is only one part of the job. I also have to manage appointments, communicate with clients, keep people coming back, handle expenses, and make sure the experience I provide reflects well on my business.

I enjoy the independence that comes with working for myself, but I also appreciate the responsibility. If something goes wrong, there is no one else to pass it to. That has made me more comfortable solving problems, adapting, and taking ownership of the result.

What barbering has taught me

People remember how you treat them.

Working one-on-one with clients has made communication one of the most important parts of my work. Listening, understanding what someone wants, and earning repeat business are skills I want to carry into my next career.
