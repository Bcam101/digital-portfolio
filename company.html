Company Profile

A mission I can personally connect with.

For this portfolio, I selected the Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial Association because the organization connects my military background with the type of administrative and operations work I want to continue developing.

Organization

Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial Association is a San Diego nonprofit organization whose mission centers on honoring veterans by preserving their legacies. The memorial uses individual plaques and public programs to tell the stories of service members from different eras and branches of the military.

Source: Mt. Soledad National Veterans Memorial

Preferred Geography

The role is based in San Diego, with the business office in Kearny Mesa and occasional work connected to the memorial in La Jolla. San Diego is a location I am interested in professionally, and the area's large military and veteran community makes the organization's mission especially relevant to me.

Why This Organization Appeals to Me

As an Air Force veteran, I connect personally with an organization built around preserving veterans' stories and recognizing military service. I also like that the Administrative & Operations Coordinator position is hands-on and varied. It combines organization, customer and donor communication, records, events, and day-to-day problem solving. Those responsibilities line up well with the parts of my military and business experience that I have enjoyed most.

Comparable Organizations

Organizations serving a similar audience or competing for visitors, donors, volunteers, and community attention include the USS Midway Museum, the Veterans Museum at Balboa Park, and other San Diego-area military heritage and veterans organizations. Mt. Soledad stands out to me because its focus is personal: individual plaques preserve the photographs, stories, and accomplishments of veterans rather than only presenting military history at a broader level.

What I Would Bring

I would bring experience with administrative documentation, customer service, scheduling, records, inventory, Microsoft Office, and working independently. My Air Force background also gives me a direct understanding of the community the organization serves, while running my own business has made me comfortable handling changing priorities and taking ownership of day-to-day needs.
